Platform Philosophy
Core Thesis: Cyber resilience is not only a technology problem. It is fundamentally a people, capability, and readiness problem — and those dimensions demand the same executive intelligence as any other strategic risk.
Innovation Angle: This platform bridges the gap between HR intelligence and cyber risk management — positioning workforce data as a first-class input to enterprise security strategy.
Design Principle: Executive-grade. Data-dense. Actionable. Every data point in this platform answers one question: what should leadership decide next?
