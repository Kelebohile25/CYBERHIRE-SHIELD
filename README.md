## About This Project

CYBERHIRE SHIELD™ is an executive-grade, browser-ready workforce cyber resilience intelligence platform — independently conceptualised, designed, and directed by **Kelebohile Mokoena** as a portfolio demonstration.

The platform explores the intersection of:

- **Cybersecurity** — workforce exposure, risk scoring, capability gaps
- **Talent Acquisition** — hiring intelligence, pipeline analytics, sourcing
- **Workforce Intelligence** — people readiness, succession depth, attrition risk
- **People Analytics** — capability mapping, certification tracking, training readiness
- **AI & Future of Work** — simulation, prediction, and executive decision support

## Core Thesis

> Cyber resilience is not only a technology problem.  
> It is fundamentally a **people, capability, and readiness** problem.

This platform positions workforce data as a first-class input to enterprise security strategy — giving CISOs, CHROs, CROs, and Boards a unified intelligence view of their most critical risk surface: the people who run their cyber defence.
